About Me

I am the CEO of Calpine Group, a group of companies based out of Infopark, Kochi, with offices in Silicon Valley (California), Austin (Texas), and Technopark, Trivandrum. I also serve as Chairman of GTECH — the Group of Technology Companies — the association of IT and ITES companies in Kerala.

Calpine Group is focused on Life Sciences research, developing products and services for companies such as Merck, PerkinElmer, Agilent, and Beckman Coulter for their drug research. I've led this effort for the past 23 years, being among the first companies established at Infopark, Kochi.

Before founding Calpine Group, I spent about 15 years in Silicon Valley, California in senior management positions as a Scientist and Software Architect for companies including Hewlett-Packard and Agilent Technologies. I was also part of Caliper Life Sciences, a startup known for innovative microfluidic products in the genomics market.
